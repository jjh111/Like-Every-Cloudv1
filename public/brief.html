Like Every Cloud

a Chadian cassette shop · past + present

Like Every Cloud is an interactive 3D scene built around a small cassette shop in Chad — a workspace where the same walls hold two eras at once. In the past, the shop ran on tape: a stack of handwritten cassettes, a boombox, a generator outside humming through the afternoon. In the present, the same room is given over to sewing. The cassettes are still there, but it's the sewing machine's soft purr that now structures the air. You move between the two states the way the day moves between weather — slowly, by feeling. The work exists to preserve rare Chadian and Sudanese recordings inside the place they belong, and to give a visitor somewhere to be for a while: not a tour, not a puzzle, just a room with a long memory and the music still playing.
